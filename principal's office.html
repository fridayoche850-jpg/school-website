principal's/ceo messages
Our strength lies in the creativity, ingenuity and experience of our Managemen Team and the relentless support of the owners. Our Teachers are motivated to function at their very best in the execution of their duties. Hence, both staff and the Senior Management Team work smarter; rather than harder under my leadership to support all areas of need for growth and development of the school.

We provide opportunities outside the classroom through extra-curricular activities such as sports and club activities; not leaving out educational excursions both within the country and abroad. We believe that out-of-the-classroom exposure would build students’ stamina, improve creativity and stabilize their focus. Some of the activities also provide a veritable platform for leadership training for them as well. Our two decades of academic success story has deservedly earned us the recognition as the school of first choice by parents within
and around Abuja. Having worked with the school in different capacities, deputizing to four different Principals and acting as Principal on several occasions in the past, my elevation as Principal/CEO on the 1st of October, 2022 only marks a significant turn in the administrative history of the school to which I’m glad to be part. As a seasoned administrator myself, I have my administrative blueprint which is to digitalize the school. In my vision, I can foresee a Capital Science

Academy that enjoys 24/7 electricity through solar energy, a digital supervision mechanism through CCTV, digitalized classrooms powered by smart/interactive boards – all culminating into a 100% success in all our internal and external examinations. I trust that within the next few years of our operation under my watch in (now) the heart of Abuja– having successfully completed a relocation from Kuje to Kado, my vision will be accomplished to the glory of God and the benefit of man: the students, the parents, the staff and the larger society.
