school Curriculum

the techbase institution offers a super standards–based curriculum which incorporate the student acquisition of concepts and the development of skills for students in preschool through Grade 12. The standards which guide our curriculum development are those recommended by professional organizations across the nigeria and all over the state. All instruction, with the exception of foreign language classes, is presented in English. Th school conducts standardized tests annually to ensure the quality of the education received by the students, using the NWEA MAP Growth Tests for students in Grades 2-10. The high school students take the PSAT, SAT, and AP exams.
