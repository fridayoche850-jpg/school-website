TOP NOTCHED ADMINISTRATION AT techbase institution ABUJA

To start with, September 2022 was the beginning of another glorious academic year for techbase institution, Abuja because of the much talk about happened. It was when CSA moved from Kuje to the heart of FCT-Abuja, to provide the usual quality education in a more serene and secure environment.

techbase institution, Abuja, is blissful in its new location in BUA Estate, Kado Abuja, to continue providing excellence in education and stimulating creativity in its students and perpetuating the production of global leaders for the future.

Furthermore, techbase institution is in the city to provide urban education to parents who elect day and boarding education through investment in information and Communication Technology (ICT)

The school leverages the new location to go all out for ICT and technological super highway; to ensure sustainable learning in the 21st Century. 21st-century education is about giving students the knowledge, skills and right attitudes they need to succeed in the innovative world; and helping them grow the confidence to practice the acquired education to benefit themselves and the world at large. CSA Abuja is harnessing and facilitating the learning in the students, with numerous information at their fingertips.

Secondly, at the moment, techbase institution abuja has a new management setting. Mrs Rachel C. L Borland, the former Principal/CEO,is now replaced with Alh Bunuyaminu Bello Yero as th e new Principal/CEO. The new set of the management team are men of impeccable character, educators of excellence experien ces and track records of greatness in educating students.

The new management came on board with five-point agenda to further enhance quality education in the school, explained below.

Use of solar power as a source of electricity for uninterrupted power supply to the school Deployment of smart boards to the classrooms to facilitate a seamless learning system Use of CCTV cameras for monitoring and security of students Introduction of laptops, tablets, and other digital devices to improve learning experience. To move from paper pencil assessment to a computer-based testing system. With the above, the management intended to achieve 100% A-C pass rates in all subjects and all examinations through IT and IT super high-way learning experiences. Thirdly, the techbase institution Abuja has 21st-century teachers with accompa nying attributes, ensuring that educational strategies are implemented. In addition, all the administrative staff are up to date in providing the necessary support to the teaching staff whilst the ambience of teamwork among staff is the school anthem.

Furthermore, the school is investing heavily in sporting equipment and leveraging the large expanse of sports arena provided at the BUA Estate that makes the sporting a cardinal point in the school and a recreational tool for the students.

Lastly, numerous facilities abound in the school for the students’ enduring learning. The school has well- equipped laboratories, computer laboratories, spaced classrooms, a hygienic environment, well-treated water and food for students’ well-being and provides clinical services to look after the health of our students. The security arrangement both for the BUA Estate in general and school, in particular, are top-notch.

You have indeed made a wise choice by choosing techbase institution, Abuja for educating your wards, welcome onboard.
