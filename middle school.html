The techbase institution abuja is designed to develop well rounded, confident and responsible students and pupils who aspire to achieve their full potentials. Our curriculum is designed to make a progressive transition from the early childhood through Basic Primary and Junior Secondary to Senior Secondary School smooth as possible.

Hence, we are determined to promote practical based activities through the structure of our learning stages to ensure that students in the School learn in a friendly, safe and motivating environment. Our basic primary curriculum is designed to inculcate the knowledge of Literacy, Numeracy, the ability to Communicate effectively and laying a sound basic for scientific, critical and reflective thinking. Curriculum

Our JSS curriculum is designed to make the transition from Junior Secondary to Senior Secondary school smooth as possible. During the three years in Junior school, students study all 19 subjects contained in the National Curriculum. These include:

student support

An extra class program is available to give support to students who our differentiation systems indicated as in need of extra help. Support classes are small (not more than 12 students) and individual programs in English, Mathematics, Science and technology are tailored to meet the needs of each student. All students are guided through the progress of self – assessment and target setting and regular reviews which take place between the teacher and the students in the support program.

Basic education Certificate examination and education resources centre / NECO

As in all work at techbase institution elements such as Information and Communication Technology, Literacy and Numeracy, Health Education, Enterprise Skills, Problem-Solving skills and Study Skills run through the curriculum leading to the NECO – Basic Education Certificate Examination and Education Resource Centre for the award of JSS – Junior Secondary School Certificate at end of JSS3
