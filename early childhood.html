school academic program

The techbase institution abuja is designed to develop well rounded, confident and responsible students and pupils who aspire to achieve their full potentials.

Our curriculum is designed to make a progressive transition from the earlychildhood through Basic Primary and Junior Secondary to Senior Secondary School smooth as possible. Hence, we are determined to promote practical based activities through the structure of our learning stages to ensure that students in the School learn in a friendly, safe and motivating environment.

Early childhood – comprises of

  • Nursery 1 ———————–2 to 3 years
  • Nursery 2 ———————–3 to 4 years
  • Reception ———————- 4 to 5 years

early childhood

The Early childhood Foundation Stage curriculum sets standards for the learning, development and care of children from birth to 5 years old. The early childhood is aimed at supporting the learning and development of children from birth.

Typically, Nursery and Reception will cater from children aged between 2/3 and 5 years old.

the following are the list of their subject areas:

  • Literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Understanding the world
  • Expressive Arts and Design.

our PRIME AREAS:

i. Personal, Social and Emotional Development
ii. Communication and Language
iii. Physical Development
All of these areas are connected and it describes universal aspects of Early Childhood Development.

DISCIPLINE

We have zero tolerance to bullying. Our students are taught to

  • Respect all staff
  • Respect our environment
  • Respect school uniform
  • Respect each other
  • Respect school properties and their books

ASSESSMENT

Assessment is an integral part of the teaching process at Capital Science Academy; we include assessment in all our learners’ activities both formally and informally.

However, we also provide and use a framework for continuous assessment, as an integral part of the teaching program and allows us to provide parents with a balanced assessment of their child’s progress.

student support

An extra class program is available to give support to students who our differentiation systems indicated as in need of extra help. Support classes are small (not more than 12 students) and individual programs in English, Mathematics, Science and technology are tailored to meet the needs of each student. All students are guided through the progress of self – assessment and target setting and regular reviews which take place between the teacher and the students in the support program.

Basic education certifiCATE examination and education resources centre / NECO

As in all work at techbase institution elements such as Information and Communication Technology, Literacy and Numeracy, Health Education, Enterprise Skills, Problem-Solving skills and Study Skills run through the curriculum leading to the NECO – Basic Education Certificate Examination and Education Resource Centre for the award of JSS – Junior Secondary School Certificate at end of JSS3
