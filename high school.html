techbase institution abuja is an offshoot of the prestigious Science and art Academy, Kuje Abuja. It is known for her long history of academic excellence and moral uprightness. techbase institution ranks among top 5 schools in Abuja and Nigeria in general. We have produced a large number of graduates who have gone to study both in Nigeria and foreign universities all over the world and have excelled as most of them graduated with first class honours in different disciplines.

all Our ex-students have involved themselves in the various field and department such as engineering, law, medicine, administration, business, petroleum, aviation, etc
